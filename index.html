Why a webinar needs a second life on paper

A webinar is an hour of work you've already paid for. Most of its value evaporates the moment the recording stops getting watched.

01

The recording stops working

Most registrants never rewatch a webinar replay. A guide keeps the content earning its keep for months, not days.

02

It's a lead magnet, already made

The material exists. Turning it into a gated PDF gives your team a new download to run ads against — without a new content budget.

03

It travels where video can't

A PDF gets forwarded in Slack, dropped in a proposal, printed for a meeting. A video link mostly doesn't.

How it works
